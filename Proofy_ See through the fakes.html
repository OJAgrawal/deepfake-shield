FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Is a Proofy score proof that something is fake?

No. Scores are probabilities from detection models. They can be wrong, especially on new generation methods or heavily compressed media. Use them as one input alongside source checks.

What does Proofy analyze?

Images, audio clips, and pasted or highlighted text. Each type runs through its own check, and the results appear together in the popup.

Where does the analysis run?

On the Proofy backend. In the local setup, that's a FastAPI server on your own machine, so you decide where media is processed.

Does it work on every website?

It works wherever the extension can read the page, and you can always upload media directly into the popup. Some pages restrict extensions, so the popup is the fallback.

🦊

Check before you share.

Free to try. Proofy gives a probabilistic signal, not proof. Always confirm important claims with trusted sources.

Download Proofy for Chrome
